# NutriPack
NutriPack — AI-powered food packaging recommendation and optimization system that analyzes food properties, storage conditions, transport risks, cost, and sustainability to suggest suitable packaging solutions.

NutriPack is an AI-powered food packaging recommendation system that intelligently matches food products with suitable packaging based on safety, storage, transport, cost, and sustainability factors.

Our goal: Optimize food packaging with data-driven recommendations that reduce waste, improve shelf life, and support sustainable packaging decisions.
